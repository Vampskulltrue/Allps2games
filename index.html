✦ what is this place?

Allps2randoms is a fan-made love letter to the PS2 era: glossy discs, strange licensed games, Japanese-only releases, forgotten sequels and late-night “what even is this game?” discoveries. This version starts with a curated list and tries to expand it using Wikipedia's public category API.

When a Wikipedia article has a thumbnail, the site uses it as cover art. Not every game has an image, and some covers are copyrighted; the article link is provided for context and source. Wikimedia image licensing varies, so check the source page before reusing artwork elsewhere.

No rankings. No gatekeeping. Just spin the disc. ★
